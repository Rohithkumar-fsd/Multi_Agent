BANKING FAQ

Savings Account

A savings account allows customers to deposit, store and withdraw money.
The account balance can be viewed through the banking application.

KYC

KYC stands for Know Your Customer.
Customers may be required to provide valid identity and address documents
during the KYC process.

Transaction History

Customers can view transactions associated with their own accounts.
Transaction information includes transaction amount, type, status and date.

Account Security

Customers must not share their passwords, PINs or authentication tokens
with anyone.

Account Support

For account-related issues, customers can contact the bank's official
support channel.
